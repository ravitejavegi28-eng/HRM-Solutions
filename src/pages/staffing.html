Define the role before starting the search.

A useful hiring brief goes beyond a job title. It explains what the person will do, which skills are essential, where they will work, and how the hiring decision will be made.

From our Jersey City contact point, we discuss requirements for roles across the United States. Share the role's work location, your interview process, and target start date so the conversation is specific to your needs.

We partner with HR managers and directors, talent acquisition and recruiting managers, CIOs, CTOs, IT directors, procurement teams, and business owners. Our approach emphasizes relevant candidates, responsiveness, and long-term working relationships.

Three ways to build your technology team.

Contract staffing

Contract-to-hire staffing

Direct-hire recruitment

Find candidates for permanent technology positions. We align sourcing and screening with the responsibilities, experience, and technical requirements of your team.

What to include in a job requirement

  • Role title, responsibilities, and number of openings
  • Essential skills and relevant experience
  • US work location and onsite, hybrid, or remote arrangement
  • Compensation range and intended engagement type
  • Target start date, interview stages, and hiring contact

Before you enquire

Is this an employer hiring enquiry?

Yes. The hiring form is for businesses sharing open roles or staffing requirements. For a career enquiry, email info@hrm-solutions.com with a short introduction.

Do I need a complete job description?

A short outline is enough to begin. Include the role, location, essential skills, and timing. The next conversation can cover the remaining details.

How are fees and timelines decided?

They depend on the role and agreed service scope. Request a discussion of pricing and timing for your requirement before committing to work.
