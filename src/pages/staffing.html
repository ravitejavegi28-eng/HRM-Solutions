What to include in a job requirement

  • Role title, responsibilities, and number of openings
  • Essential skills and relevant experience
  • US work location and onsite, hybrid, or remote arrangement
  • Compensation range and intended engagement type
  • Target start date, interview stages, and hiring contact

Before you enquire

Is this an employer hiring enquiry?

Yes. The hiring form is for businesses sharing open roles or staffing requirements. For a career enquiry, email info@hrm-solutions.com with a short introduction.

Do I need a complete job description?

A short outline is enough to begin. Include the role, location, essential skills, and timing. The next conversation can cover the remaining details.

How are fees and timelines decided?

They depend on the role and agreed service scope. Request a discussion of pricing and timing for your requirement before committing to work.
