Resources

Make your next step a clear one.

Practical starting points for choosing HR software, preparing your records, and briefing a recruiting partner.

Migration checklist

Moving from Excel to HRM

Prepare your records before moving to a new system.

Read the checklist
  1. List the spreadsheets, systems, and people that own your HR records.
  2. Remove duplicate records and agree a unique employee ID.
  3. Reconcile leave balances, reporting lines, and work locations.
  4. Define access permissions and approval responsibilities.
  5. Test a small import, check totals, and agree a cutover date with a backup plan.
Demo guide

Choosing HR software

Use your actual workflows to guide the conversation.

Read the guide
  1. Choose three tasks that create the most manual work.
  2. Ask to see each task from employee request to manager approval.
  3. Confirm supported locations, devices, and integrations.
  4. Discuss permissions, data exports, backups, and support arrangements.
  5. Compare the full cost of setup, migration, training, and recurring service.
Hiring brief

Brief your recruiting partner

A specific role brief makes candidate evaluation clearer.

Read the brief
  1. Describe the role's responsibilities and expected outcomes.
  2. Separate essential skills from skills that can be learned.
  3. Specify the work location, schedule, and remote or onsite expectations.
  4. Agree the compensation range, target start date, and interview stages.
  5. Name the hiring decision-maker and agree how feedback will be shared.
Operations checklist

Prepare payroll inputs

Check the source information before your payroll review.

Read the checklist
  1. Confirm joiners, leavers, effective dates, and approved salary changes.
  2. Resolve attendance exceptions, leave, and overtime approvals.
  3. Review variable pay, reimbursements, and deductions with the responsible team.
  4. Compare totals with the previous period and investigate unexpected changes.
  5. Have your payroll owner confirm local requirements and final approval.
